Alternative Fuel Types

Biodiesel

Biodiesel is a renewable fuel made from vegetable oils, animal fats, or recycled cooking grease. It can power diesel engines with lower emissions than traditional petroleum diesel.

Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging

EV charging stations provide electricity to recharge battery-powered vehicles. They range from slow Level 1 chargers to fast DC chargers for rapid charging.

Hydrogen Fuel

Hydrogen stations dispense compressed hydrogen gas used by fuel-cell vehicles. These vehicles generate electricity from hydrogen, producing only water vapor as exhaust.

E85 (Flex Fuel)

E85 is a high-ethanol blend (up to 85% ethanol) used in flex-fuel vehicles. It burns cleaner than gasoline and is typically made from corn or other plant materials.
