About

Our project aims to create a web platform that provides users with access to alternative fueling station locations. This tool will support travelers and everyday alternative-fuel users by helping them locate and navigate to stations that meet their needs. Users may search using their current location or any specified address, with the ability to define a custom search radius and apply filters relevant to their fueling requirements. Selecting a station will reveal detailed information, including connector types, connector counts, and fuel classifications. While our dataset contains station information across the United States and portions of Canada, our initial implementation will center on the Washington region. As the project progresses, we intend to widen our geographic scope outside of Washington State and increase with advancements in functionality, data processing, and system capabilities.
